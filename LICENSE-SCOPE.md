# What the license covers

The [LICENSE](LICENSE) file is the verbatim CC BY 4.0 legal text, unmodified so that
automated license detectors recognise it. This file records what that license does
and does not extend to, which the license text itself cannot say.

## Covered: CC BY 4.0

Level's own aggregates, the index scores and subscores, and the compilation. Free to
share and adapt for any purpose including commercially, with attribution to Level
(https://levelcfo.com) and an indication of whether changes were made.

## Not covered: the cited sources

The `source` columns cite third-party publications: the U.S. Bureau of Labor
Statistics, SEC filings, CFMA, IBISWorld, trade associations, and others. Those
underlying figures belong to their publishers and are **not ours to license**. The
citation is there so you can consult the original.

Attribution under CC BY 4.0 covers this compilation, not the sources it cites. If you
reuse a specific third-party figure at scale, check that publisher's own terms.
